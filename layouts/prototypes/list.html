For John

The signup form posts to /api/subscribe and appends the address to a Cloudflare KV namespace bound as SIGNUPS. One key per email. A hidden honeypot field is ignored. After a real signup the page comes back with a confirmation: “You're on the list. You'll be the first to hear about new releases.”

Export the list as CSV once EXPORT_TOKEN is set on the Pages project:

curl -sS -H "Authorization: Bearer $EXPORT_TOKEN" \
  https://realjrk.com/api/export -o signups.csv

Setup, the local Wrangler preview, and a second way to read the keys are in functions/README.md. Nothing here is deployed, and Cloudflare is unchanged.
